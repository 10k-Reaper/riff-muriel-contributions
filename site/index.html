    Get Muriel

    Always the newest build, straight from GitHub.

    Android

    1. Download Muriel.apk on your phone.
    2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
    3. Tap Install. Updates install over it and keep your music.
    Download APK

    iPhone

    1. Install SideStore once (needs a computer one time).
    2. Tap Add to SideStore. Muriel installs and keeps itself updated.
    Add to SideStore Download .ipa

    No SideStore? Install the .ipa with Sideloadly from your computer. iPhones only run signed apps, so one of these is needed.

    Windows

    1. Download Muriel.exe. It's portable, no installer.
    2. Run it. If SmartScreen appears: More info → Run anyway.
    Download for Windows

    Or install it with a desktop shortcut from PowerShell:

    $ProgressPreference='SilentlyContinue'; $p="$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\Muriel"; ni -ItemType Directory -Force $p | Out-Null; iwr https://github.com/cloverscripts/riff-muriel/releases/download/windows-latest/Muriel.exe -OutFile "$p\Muriel.exe"; Unblock-File "$p\Muriel.exe"; $s=(New-Object -ComObject WScript.Shell).CreateShortcut("$([Environment]::GetFolderPath('Desktop'))\Muriel.lnk"); $s.TargetPath="$p\Muriel.exe"; $s.IconLocation="$p\Muriel.exe,0"; $s.Save()

    Mac & Linux

    1. Pick your download below (v1.1.4).
    2. Mac: if it's blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

    About

    Muriel is a free music player made by cloverscripts and based on Riff by rootscripts (MIT). No ads, no account, no subscription, and nobody earns money from it.

    It's an independent, non-commercial open-source project: it hosts no music and plays publicly available sources on your own device. Use it for private listening and only download music you have the right to.

    Questions

    Is Muriel really free?

    Yes. No ads, no account, no subscription. It's open source and based on Riff by rootscripts (MIT).

    Is Muriel connected to YouTube, SoundCloud or Spotify?

    No. Muriel is an independent, non-commercial open-source player and hosts no music; it plays publicly available sources on your own device. Use it for private listening and only download music you have the right to. More in the legal notice, which also says how rights holders can reach us.

    Where does the download counter come from?

    Straight from GitHub: it adds up every download of every Muriel file there and refreshes while this page is open. Downloads from before the counter existed are included where GitHub still had them.

    Why can't I just tap "install" on iPhone?

    iOS only runs apps signed with a certificate from Apple. Muriel doesn't use leaked certificates (those get revoked and can push anything to your phone), so on iPhone it installs through SideStore or Sideloadly with your own Apple ID.

    Why does my iPhone app stop opening after 7 days?

    Apps signed with a free Apple ID expire after 7 days. SideStore refreshes them automatically on your phone; with Sideloadly, just install again.

    Does music keep playing with the screen off?

    Yes, on iPhone and Android, with play, pause and skip on the lock screen.

    Where are my downloaded songs?

    Inside Muriel (Library → Offline). On iPhone they're also in the Files app under Muriel.
